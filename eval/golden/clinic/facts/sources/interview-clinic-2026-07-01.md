# Interview: Practice Manager, Novak Family Clinic

**Date:** 2026-07-01
**Participant:** Practice Manager
**Scope:** appointment booking and patient records

---

Patients book appointments through the online booking portal or by calling the front
desk.

The electronic health record system holds all patient charts and treatment notes. The
booking portal writes confirmed appointments into the EHR through a scheduling
interface that the EHR provides.

Invoices are produced by the billing module inside the EHR; we do not run a separate
billing system.

The booking portal is hosted by an external provider. The EHR runs on a server in the
back office, and the practice manager worries that nobody has tested its backup
restore.
