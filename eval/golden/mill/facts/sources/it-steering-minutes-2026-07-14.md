BRIGHTWATER MILLS LTD
IT Steering Committee – minutes of the meeting held on 14 July 2026
CONFIDENTIAL. Internal use only. Do not forward outside Brightwater Mills.
Present: Helen Ward (Operations Director, chair), Raj Patel (IT Manager), Tom Okafor
(Finance Controller), Ana Silva (Quality Lead).
Apologies: none.
Agenda: 1. Production planning. 2. Quality data. 3. Orders and finance. 4. Plant
infrastructure. 5. Any other business.
1. Production planning
Raj Patel: The production planning tool MillPlan schedules every milling run. MillPlan
sends the daily production plan to the plant control system at 05:00 each morning.
Helen Ward asked whether the plan can be changed after it is sent.
Raj Patel: Changes after 05:00 are phoned through to the shift supervisor and are not
recorded in MillPlan.
2. Quality data
Ana Silva: Every batch is tested in the lab before it is released. Lab results are
recorded in LabTrack. LabTrack is a spreadsheet-based tool, and it is maintained by one
person in the quality team.
